Superteam NL exists to change that — the official Dutch builders' hub for the Solana ecosystem.

01 / The problem

The Dutch web3 builder problem.

Talent is here. Infrastructure to turn that talent into shipped projects on Solana isn't.
01
Students don't know where to start
330,000+ Dutch university students. No structured on-ramp into Solana — most never even hear about it.
02
Early builders lack support
Curious devs and designers can't find mentors, technical help, or peers building in the same direction.
03
The local ecosystem is fragmented
Solana projects in NL exist in isolation. No shared rooms, no shared distribution, no shared deal flow.
04
No pipeline from curiosity to shipping
No clear path from "I want to learn" to "I've shipped an MVP" to "I'm building a company."

05 / Timing

Why this works now.

This wasn't possible in 2021. It is now.
AI Building got 10× cheaper Solo builders ship in days what teams used to ship in quarters. The barrier to MVP collapsed.
Infra Solana finally matured Token Extensions, Firedancer, restaking primitives. The rails for serious consumer apps are here.
UX Crypto stopped feeling like crypto Embedded wallets, gasless flows, fiat on-ramps. Builders can target normal users now.
Talent Students want to ship, not intern A new generation of NL students would rather found a company than join a Big 4 grad scheme.
Europe EU lacks local builder ecosystems The Bay Area and Lagos have rooms full of crypto founders. Most EU cities don't. The gap is the opportunity.
Scale Small teams ship globally Distribution is one good launch away. NL builders are no longer constrained by NL market size.
